Hi everyone, this past month I had the opportunity of mentoring for 2 days an awesome group of community members from 
[.NET Dominicana](<https://www.meetup.com/dotnetdom/>)
 on how to practice and prepare to accomplish their goal of obtaining the Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) Certification.

<a id="more-2288"></a>

**Event name:**
.NET Dominicana – Exam Prep Sessions For AZ-900

**Talk Title:**
 Azure For Devs – Exam Prep Sessions For AZ-900

**Description:**
 How can we practice, prepare and what resources are available to accomplish the goal of obtaining the Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) Certification.

**Language:**
Spanish

**Attendees:**
25~

**Date 1st Session:**
Friday 12 March, 2021 From 7:00PM – 9:00PM

**Date 2nd Session:**
Saturday 13 March, 2021 From 3:00PM – 6:00PM

---

#### **TOPICS DISCUSSED ON STUDY GROUP**

<a id="block-d2bda4dc-f00a-44a0-8be1-1119423425ba"></a>

* What is AZ-900?
* Azure Fundamentals Path – Microsoft Learn
* Exam Structure
* Prepping your workstation and space for the proctored Exam
* 2 Hours of Live Practice Exams
* Pricing & How to Schedule your Exam
