Hey Guys!!

I know it’s been a long time since I last posted, had to take some time to organize a few things in these last few months, but we are now getting back at it! 😀

Even though I was not posting as frequently I tried to maintain my activity with the community and one of those was participating in the cool VS Launch Recap Party the DotNetDo Community organized, I had the opportunity of participating as a Keynote speaker for the event giving an overview of all the amazing goodies the Microsoft team brought to us with this new release.

<a id="more-1222"></a>

We got to record about 80% of the Keynote (Technical issues are always there :\{ haha) but it’s still a good chunk of information and preview of the event and we hope you enjoy it! 😀

Here’s a brief more detailed description of the event:

**Event name:**
 Visual Studio Launch Event Recap 2019 – DotNetDo

**Talk Title:**
 Keynote: What’s New – Overview

**Description:**
 We got to see and experience first hand all of the new goodies and features that VS 2019 offer to us in all the different aspect of development.

**Language:**
Spanish

**Website:**
[https://www.meetup.com/dotnetdom/events/260386435/](<https://www.meetup.com/dotnetdom/events/260386435/>)

**Attendees:**
40~

**Organizer:**
.NET Dominicana

#### **Topics discussed on the Live presentation**

* **What’s New – Overview** by Luis Pujols
* **DevOps and .NET Core** by José Ruiz
* **VS For Mac & Xamarin** by Charlin Agramonte
* **Machine Learning & VS Subscriptions** by Rendy Del Rosario
* **Azure Services & App Center** by Angel Garcia
