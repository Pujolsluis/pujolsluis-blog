Hey Guys!

On Saturday 2nd of March, I had the opportunity of participating in the 15th edition of 
[South Florida Code Camp](<https://www.fladotnet.com/codecamp/Home.aspx>)
, that is a one-day GEEK FEST community event driven by a group of dedicated volunteers and speakers. I got the chance to talk about 
**Architecture with Prism in Xamarin Forms**
.

<a id="more-1158"></a>

---

**Event name:**
 South Florida Code Camp, March 2019

**Talk Title:**
 Let’s Talk Architecture with Prism

**Description:**
I got to speak about building loosely coupled, maintainable, and testable cross-platform applications with Prism.

**Language:**
English

**Event Website:**
https://www.fladotnet.com/codecamp/Home.aspx

---

My participation was on the 
**Xamarin/Mobile Development Track**
, I got to meet the Florida Community and we learned about Prism and all it provides like an implementation of a collection of design patterns that are helpful in writing well-structured and maintainable Xamarin applications, including MVVM, dependency injection, Commands, EventAggregator, and others.

I 
**loved the organization of the event and meeting all of the cool people that came down to hang with us**
. got to meet awesome speakers from all over and amazing attendees that love to learn and share knowledge from different backgrounds I can say that 
**South Florida is certainly doing it right!**
 what a great event and community, if you ever have the chance don’t miss out on attending or speaking at this great event in the future! 😀

#### **Topics discussed on the Live presentation**

* Why think about architecture?
  * What do we need, to have a good architecture?
  * SOLID Principles.
  * Frameworks Available for improving our architecture
* Why Prism?
  * Prism Features?
  * How to Setup?
  * Project Structure
* Live Demo on how to use P <a id="204"></a>

  rism in our Xamarin Forms <a id="200"></a>

  App’s .
