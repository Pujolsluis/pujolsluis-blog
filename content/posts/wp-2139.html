I had the opportunity to speak at the Toronto Mobile Developers meetup, On Saturday 15th of September 2020, I got to meet with all the amazing community members and talk about Mobile App Redesign For Developers and how we implemented all our research into the GitTrends open-source application.

Event name:                                    Toronto Mobile Developers Meetup, September 2020

Talk Title:                                     Mobile App Redesign GitTrends    

Description:                                    We got to learn about Mobile App Redesign and how we leveraged tools, resources, and workflows to build the clean and immersive app design for GitTrends.

Language:                                        English

Attendees:                                        20~

Event Website:                               https://www.fladotnet.com/codecamp/Home.aspx

My talk was on how polishing and working with UI/UX design can feel intimidating and like we definitely need a Designer in our team to get out a production-ready app design. the attendees were able to learn key tools, resources, and workflow they could use to start building clean and immersive app designs and how we got to redesign the GitTrends Mobile App.

TOPICS DISCUSSED ON THE LIVE PRESENTATION

  • GitTrends and Xamarin.Forms Success Story
  • Defining Our Toolkit and Workflow
    • Gathering Inspiration
    • Choosing a visual language for our design
    • Defining your base magic numbers
    • Tools to get the job done
    • Define a Feedback loop
