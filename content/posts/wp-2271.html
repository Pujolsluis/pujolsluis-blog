I had the opportunity of participating in the Panel of LaComarca Community, alongside my Xamarin friends Charlin Agramonte (XamGirl), Rendy Del Rosario(XamBoy), and Angel Garcia On Wednesday 4th of March 2021, which was a great panel focused on learning all the key concepts, and resources we need to become successful Xamarin Developers and how do we see the market for cross-platform mobile development in the future.

Event name:                                   Panel – Que necesitas para convertirte en un Xamarin Developers – LaComarca Community

Talk Title:                                    Panel – Que necesitas para convertirte en un Xamarin Developers

Description:                                    Learn key concepts, resources, and paths we can use to become confident Xamarin Profesionals. Evaluating the market for cross-platform mobile development with Xamarin in the near future.

Language:                                        Spanish

Attendees:                                        40~

Website:                                        https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UqtwY097yp8

TOPICS DISCUSSED ON THE LIVE INTERVIEW

  • What is Xamarin?
  • How did each panelist start with Cross-platform mobile development with Xamarin?
  • What do you need to be a Xamarin Developer, and how the fundamentals of Software Engineering are what we should be focusing on.
  • Xamarin vs Other Cross-Platform Technologies
  • Xamarin in the Real-world, what companies use it and for what?
  • What are the resources and paths we can follow to become confident Xamarin Developers?
  • What is .NET MAUI and how does it affect us as Xamarin Developers?
