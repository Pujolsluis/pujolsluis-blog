I had the opportunity of participating in the DCI Annual Conference in the Dominicana Republic, On Saturday 19th of December 2020, which is a platform to present interesting ideas, experiences, and projects, taught by the most outstanding minds in their fields in the country, which create controversy and promote networking between tech communities. I got the chance to talk about the mental barriers we face when starting our careers and how to dominate our path in tech.

<a id="more-2223"></a>

---

**Event name:**
 DCI Christmas Edition – Dedicated to Mental Health and wellness in tech

**Talk Title:**
Breaking Mental Barriers and Dominating your path in Tech

**Description:**
 In this talk, the attendees got to learn about the different mental barriers everyone faces when starting their career in tech and how they can overcome them, and create a plan to create and dominate their path in tech.

**Language:**
Spanish

**Attendees:**
700+

**Website:**
[https://charlasdci.org/](<https://charlasdci.org/>)

---

#### **TOPICS DISCUSSED ON THE LIVE INTERVIEW**

* Background in Woodworking and Restaurant Industry
* Mental Barriers
  * Knowledge
  * Economic
  * Effort
  * Dreams
* My Journey
  * Coming from the woodworking and restaurant industry
  * Hosting my first community events to leverage great professionals knowledge and soak up free knowledge from the best 😛
  * Participating in ACM Programming International competition
  * Devoting myself to the local and international community by becoming a speaker and author.
  * Migrating to a new Market and thriving in my career (USA).
* Co-organize .NET Dominicana Community.
