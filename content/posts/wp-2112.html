I had the opportunity of participating in the 16th edition of South Florida Code Camp, On Saturday 29th of February 2020, which is a one-day GEEK FEST community event driven by a group of dedicated volunteers and speakers. I got the chance to talk about Mobile DevOps in Xamarin Forms.

Event name:                                    South Florida Code Camp, February 2020

Talk Title:                                     Mobile DevOps made Easy with VS App Center    

Description:                                    We got to learn about DevOps making use of all the great services Visual Studio App Center provides us, to take advantage of all the tools we need to automatically manage our Mobile DevOps.

Language:                                        English

Event Website:                               https://www.fladotnet.com/codecamp/Home.aspx

My participation was on the Xamarin/Mobile Development Track, I got to meet the Florida Community and we got to see how many tedious and manual processes we are actually doing daily as developers, how DevOps can allow us to automate them using the awesome services provided by Visual Studio App Center and how it also provides us a bunch of other goodies that allow us to have more power in our app like Crashanalitics, Analytics, Events, Push Notifications, etc.

TOPICS DISCUSSED ON THE LIVE PRESENTATION

  • What is DevOps?
  • DevOps applied to Mobile Development
  • Visual Studio App Center
    • Build
    • Testing
    • Distribute
    • App Health and CrashAnalytics
    • Analytics and User Behavior
    • Push Notifications
    • Pricing? Free for Devs!
