I had the opportunity of participating in the Xamarin Assemble annual event, On Saturday 18th of July 2020, which is the biggest Cross-Platform Mobile App development conference in Argentina an event that is organized for and by the community. I got the chance to talk about 
**Mobile App Redesign For Developers**
.

<a id="more-2118"></a>

---

**Event name:**
 Xamarin Assemble, July 2020

**Talk Title:**
 Mobile App Redesign For Developers

**Description:**
We got to learn about Mobile App Redesign and how to leverage tools, resources, and workflows to start building clean and immersive app designs.

**Language:**
English

**Attendees:**
500~

**Event Website:**
https://www.fladotnet.com/codecamp/Home.aspx

---

My participation was on how polishing and working with UI/UX design can feel intimidating and like we definitely need a Designer in our team to get out a production-ready app design. the attendees were able to learn key tools, resources, and workflow they could use to start building clean and immersive app designs.

#### **TOPICS DISCUSSED ON THE LIVE PRESENTATION**

* GitTrends and Xamarin.Forms Success Story
* Defining Our Toolkit and Workflow
  * Gathering Inspiration
  * Choosing a visual language for our design
  * Defining your base magic numbers
  * Tools to get the job done
  * Define a Feedback loop
